THE HUMAN VARIABLE

The recipe matters. The baker matters too.

Two people can follow the same recipe and still create different bread. A baker feels the dough, judges fermentation by sight and touch, adjusts for the weather, shapes with a different rhythm and decides exactly when the loaf is ready for the oven.

01 Touch — Dough can feel tight, elastic, wet or ready.
02 Timing — Experience helps a baker know when fermentation has gone far enough.
03 Instinct — Small decisions turn a standard recipe into a signature loaf.
